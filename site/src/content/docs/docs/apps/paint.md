title: Paint
description: Draw and edit pictures with brushes, shapes, text, fill, selections, crop, resize and rotate.

A familiar paint program for PNG, JPEG, WebP and BMP pictures (it also opens GIF and ICO). Opening a picture file opens
it in Paint.

## Tools

- **Pencil**, **brush** and **eraser** (to color 2 or to transparent).
- **Fill** with a tolerance, and a **color picker**.
- **Text** with font, size, bold and italic.
- **Shapes**: line, arrow, rectangle, rounded rectangle, ellipse and triangle, as an outline, filled, or both. Hold
  **Shift** for a square, circle or 45° line.

Left click paints with color 1, right click with color 2. **X** swaps them; **[** and **]** change the size.

## Selections

Drag a rectangle to select. Then move it (it leaves color 2 or transparency behind), press **Delete**, copy, cut, or
crop to it. **Ctrl+A** selects everything. Pictures copied from other apps paste as a movable selection, and you can drop
a picture file on the canvas to place it.

## Image menu

Resize, change the canvas size, rotate, flip and invert colors.

## Zoom and pan

**Ctrl+wheel** zooms at the cursor; hold **Space** or use the middle button to pan.

## Generate a picture with AI

With an image service set up (Settings → AI → API → Images), **Generate** draws a picture from a description with fal.ai
(FLUX, Recraft) or OpenAI (GPT Image, DALL·E), into the selection or the whole canvas. It arrives as a selection you can
move before keeping it. See [AI sidebar](/docs/ai/sidebar/) for setting up keys.

## Saving

Saving keeps the file's format. A GIF or ICO is saved as a new PNG, JPEG, WebP or BMP file.
