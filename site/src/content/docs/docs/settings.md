Open Settings with the gear at the bottom left, **Ctrl+,** or **Ctrl+K → Settings**. The sections are listed down the
left side.

## General

- **Appearance:** Light, Dark, or System (follows your computer).
- **Your name:** shown on your [board comments](/docs/apps/board/) and to the people you
  [share a document](/docs/sharing/live-sharing/) with.
- **Recent files:** the list on Home and in the command palette. **Clear** empties it.

## Apps

Turns apps on or off. A turned-off app disappears from Home, the sidebar, the command palette and the **New** menu, but
its files still open when you double-click one. To-do, HTML, Calculator, Meme Maker, Images, the API client and JSON
start turned off. See [First run](/docs/first-run/#choosing-your-apps).

## Files

- **Library:** where your documents live, `Documents/Simple Desk` to start with. **Change location…** can move your
  files to the new place.
- **Locations:** the other folders shown in the Files panel. Removing one only removes it from the panel; nothing is
  deleted. (Desktop only.)

See [First run](/docs/first-run/) and [Using several computers](/docs/several-computers/).

## Sharing

The server used for [live sharing](/docs/sharing/live-sharing/), and the documents you're sharing or have joined, with
**Stop sharing**.

## AI

Two tabs, with the **Activity** list under both:

- **API:** your **Model services** (Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, xAI, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible server) for the
  [AI sidebar](/docs/ai/sidebar/), and **Images** services for Paint's Generate.
- **MCP:** lets [agents on your computer](/docs/ai/agents/), like Claude Desktop, Claude Code and Codex, work in Simple
  Desk: **Enable MCP server**, what agents may do, which apps they can use, and how to connect each agent. (Desktop
  only.)
- **Activity:** every change an AI made since the app started, with **Show** and **Undo**. See
  [Activity and undo](/docs/ai/activity/).

## About

The version you have, and [updates](/docs/updates/): **Check now**, **Update and restart** when an update is ready, and
whether to check automatically.
